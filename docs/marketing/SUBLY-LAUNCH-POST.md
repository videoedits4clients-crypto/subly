## Long-form version (LinkedIn / Discord / community announcement)

**Introducing SUBLY — professional subtitles, processed locally on Windows.**

SUBLY is a Windows desktop app for creators, editors, and anyone who needs clean, styled captions without sending their footage to the cloud. It transcribes your video locally (English and Hindi supported today, plus Auto Detect), gives you a real word-level caption editor with full timeline control, and exports burned-in MP4 captions plus SRT/VTT/TXT — all on your own machine.

**What makes it different:**
- Transcription runs on your PC, not a cloud API — your video stays local.
- A genuine word-level editor: reorder, split, merge, and style individual words, with automatic word-by-word highlighting.
- A real timeline, not just a text box — drag, snap, zoom, and adjust caption timing directly.
- No per-minute pricing, no watermark — free during this preview period.

SUBLY is available now for Windows. Version 0.1.20.
